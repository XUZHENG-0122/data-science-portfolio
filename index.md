## Project Showcase

### Crime Patterns and Public Transport Accessibility in Greater London
**Research Question:** How does public transport accessibility (PTAL) correlate with recorded crime rates across different London boroughs, and what are the specific crime category breakdowns in high-crime areas?

**Key Visualization:**
![Top 10 London Boroughs by Crime Rate](https://github.com/XUZHENG-0122/data-science-project/blob/main/Figure_2.png?raw=true)

**Findings:** 
There is a strong positive correlation (r = 0.86) between the area-weighted PTAL score and the total crime rate. Boroughs with higher public transport accessibility tend to have higher crime rates, with "Theft" being the most dominant crime category in these central/high-PTAL areas (e.g., Westminster).
